Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how DoIt Club collects, uses and shares personal information when you use our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information you provide (e.g., name, email, profile information) and information from your activity on the platform (e.g., clubs joined, events attended).

2. How We Use Information

  • To provide and improve the service.
  • To communicate with you about events, updates and notifications.
  • To personalize recommendations and matches.

3. Sharing & Disclosure

We may share information with third-party service providers who assist with operations. We do not sell personal data to third parties.

4. Your Choices

You can edit your profile information, manage notification preferences, and request deletion of your account. For requests, contact privacydoitclub@gmail.com.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your data. However, no system is completely secure — use good password hygiene and enable any available account protections.

6. Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy. We’ll post the updated policy here with a revised effective date.
